Acme Browser

about.html

Acme browser (only for Linux) is not for everybody.

Written in python (pyGTK/WebKit4) you can easily open it and look at the code.
It works on most websites, but will crash on certain websites.
Acme browser does block adds and some malware, but it does not sanitize, or mess with, any of the contents you open.
